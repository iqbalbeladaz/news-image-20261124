# The World in One Day — Tuesday 24 November 2026

A make.fun `news-image-20261124` entry ("Sum up 24 November's world
news in one image", $100 prize pool): one 1024×1024 picture of the
day's world news, drawn with Python + Pillow by `generate.py` and
delivered by `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only), which copies
`entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`).

## What the picture shows

Six main stories, all first reported on Tuesday 24 November 2026:

- **NATO vows space defence** — alliance chief Mark Rutte says NATO
  will defend against any threat in space, amid reports Russia is
  preparing to launch a nuclear-armed satellite.
- **ICJ: genocide of the Rohingya** — the World Court finds Myanmar
  committed genocide against the Rohingya, and fourteen countries
  publicly back the ruling.
- **Ebola teams trapped (Ituri, DR Congo)** — heavy fighting near a
  hospital traps Ebola responders and forces emergency teams to take
  cover for more than 24 hours, the WHO says.
- **Huang back as Nvidia CEO** — Jensen Huang is reinstated as chief
  executive after President Trump intervenes with the board, and
  markets surge.
- **Airstrikes kill six in Gaza** — Israeli airstrikes kill at least
  six Palestinians, including a senior Hamas internal-security chief,
  local medics and officials say.
- **Shenzhou-24 lifts off** — China launches the Shenzhou-24 mission
  carrying Pakistani astronaut Muhammad Zeeshan Ali to the Tiangong
  space station, its first foreign crew member.

And in brief: a Russian strike on Ukraine kills 2 and wounds 48 as a
new analysis puts Russia's war dead above 700,000; Iran rules out
talks until Washington meets its seven conditions while tankers hit
mines in the Strait of Hormuz; Ukrainian naval drones strike a
Russian warship off Benghazi, Libya; Malaysia prepares to return more
than 6,500 Rohingya refugees to Myanmar; and the United States loses
its measles elimination status after 25 years, with a ninth death
recorded in Pennsylvania.

Headlines were checked against the day's wires and roundups (UN News,
the BBC, Reuters via Al Arabiya, the Kyiv Independent, AP reporting
and the 24 November world-news roundups) — only stories corroborated
across several of them made the picture.
